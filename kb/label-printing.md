title: Printing labels
updated: 2026-05-02
---
Labels can be printed as 4x6 inch thermal labels or as 8.5x11 inch PDF (two labels per page).

Tested thermal printers: Rollo, Zebra ZD420 and DYMO LabelWriter 5XL. Other 4x6 thermal printers usually work but are not officially supported.

If a label prints too small or cut off, check that your printer's paper size is set to 4x6 in and that scaling is set to 100% (not "fit to page").

You can reprint any label for free within 30 days of buying it from the Shipments page. Reprinting does not create a new charge.
