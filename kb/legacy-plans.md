title: Legacy plans
updated: 2026-01-20
---
The Basic plan ($9/month, 100 shipments) was discontinued on January 15, 2026. It is no longer available to new customers.

Customers who were on Basic before that date keep their price for as long as they stay on the plan. If a Basic customer switches to another plan, they cannot switch back to Basic.
