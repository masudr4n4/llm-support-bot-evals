title: Lost or damaged packages
updated: 2026-07-01
---
Carriers are responsible for packages in transit. Without Shipping Protection, claims for lost or damaged packages must be filed directly with the carrier.

Shipping Protection is an optional add-on that costs $1.25 per $100 of declared value, with a minimum of $1.25 per package. It covers up to $5,000 per package.

Shipping Protection claims must be filed within 30 days of the ship date from Shipments > File a claim. A package is considered lost if it has no tracking updates for 10 days (domestic) or 20 days (international).

Approved claims are paid within 7 business days to your original payment method.
