title: Data retention and privacy
updated: 2026-09-10
---
Shipment and order data is kept for 24 months, then deleted automatically. You can export your shipments as CSV at any time from Reports > Export.

To delete your account and all of its data, the account owner must ask a human agent. Deletion is completed within 30 days of the request and cannot be undone.

A Data Processing Agreement (DPA) for GDPR is available on request for all plans.

Tidewell never sells your data or your customers' data.
