title: Free trial
updated: 2026-06-15
---
Every new account starts with a 14-day free trial of the Growth plan. No credit card is needed to start the trial.

The trial includes up to 50 shipments. Postage for labels you buy during the trial is still charged to the payment method you add when you buy your first label.

When the trial ends, your account is paused until you choose a plan. Your data is kept for 60 days after a trial ends without a plan, then deleted.

Each business can use the free trial once. Trials cannot be extended.
