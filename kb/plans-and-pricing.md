updated: 2026-09-01

Tidewell has three plans. Prices are in USD and billed monthly unless you choose annual billing.

- Starter: $19/month. Includes 300 shipments per month and 1 user seat.
- Growth: $59/month. Includes 2,000 shipments per month and 5 user seats.
- Scale: $199/month. Includes 10,000 shipments per month and unlimited user seats.

Overage: shipments above your plan's monthly allowance cost $0.05 each on Starter, $0.03 each on Growth and $0.02 each on Scale. Overage is billed on your next invoice.

Annual billing: paying yearly gives you 2 months free (you pay for 10 months).

You can upgrade at any time and the change takes effect immediately; you are charged a prorated amount for the rest of the billing period. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing period.

Unused shipments do not roll over to the next month.
