title: Refunds and cancellation
updated: 2026-08-20
---
You can cancel your subscription at any time from Settings > Billing > Cancel plan. Your plan stays active until the end of the current billing period.

Monthly plans: we do not give refunds or prorated credits for the remaining days of a monthly billing period.

Annual plans: if you cancel within 30 days of your annual payment, you get a prorated refund for the unused months. After 30 days, annual plans are not refundable.

Postage refunds: unused labels can be voided from the Shipments page. USPS labels can be voided within 28 days of purchase; UPS and FedEx labels within 90 days. Voided postage is returned to your Tidewell balance, not your card. USPS refunds take up to 21 days to be approved.

Disputed charges, duplicate charges, or requests for a refund outside these rules are handled by the billing team, not the assistant. Ask to be connected to a human agent.
