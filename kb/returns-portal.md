title: Returns portal
updated: 2026-06-30
---
The returns portal lets your customers start a return and print a prepaid return label themselves. It is available on the Growth and Scale plans.

You choose who pays for return postage: the store, or the customer (deducted from their refund in your store platform).

Return labels are only charged when the carrier scans them. Unused return labels cost nothing.

Returns can be limited to a window you set, from 7 to 90 days after delivery.
