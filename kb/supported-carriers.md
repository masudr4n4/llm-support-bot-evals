title: Supported carriers
updated: 2026-07-10
---
Tidewell supports these carriers: USPS, UPS, FedEx, DHL Express and Canada Post.

You can use Tidewell's discounted rates, or connect your own carrier account (UPS, FedEx and DHL Express only) to use your negotiated rates.

Tidewell does not support Amazon Shipping, OnTrac or regional courier services at this time.

Canada Post is available for shipments that start in Canada only.
