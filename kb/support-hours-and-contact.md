title: Support hours and contact options
updated: 2026-09-15
---
The Tidewell assistant is available 24/7 in the help widget.

Human agents are available by chat and email Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM US Eastern Time. Average first reply time is under 2 hours during these hours.

Phone support is available on the Scale plan only.

Requests that need a human agent include: billing disputes, account deletion, suspected account compromise, and anything the assistant cannot answer. You can ask the assistant to connect you to a human at any time.
