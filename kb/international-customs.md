title: International shipping and customs
updated: 2026-06-12
---
Tidewell fills in customs forms (CN22 or CN23) automatically for international USPS shipments, using the product details from your store.

Add an HS tariff code to each product to avoid delays. Shipments without HS codes may be held by customs.

Delivered Duty Paid (DDP), where you prepay duties and taxes for your customer, is available with DHL Express only.

Tidewell cannot ship lithium batteries, aerosols or perfume internationally.
