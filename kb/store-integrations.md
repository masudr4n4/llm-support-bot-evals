title: Store integrations
updated: 2026-07-22
---
Tidewell connects to Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce and Etsy. You can connect up to 2 stores on Starter, 10 on Growth and unlimited on Scale.

New orders sync automatically every 15 minutes. To pull orders right away, click "Sync now" on the Orders page.

When you buy a label, the tracking number is written back to the store order automatically and the order is marked as fulfilled.

Amazon Seller Central and eBay are not supported yet.
