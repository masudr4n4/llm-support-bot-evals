title: Tracking and customer notifications
updated: 2026-08-05
---
Tidewell sends tracking emails to your customers when a label is created, when the package is out for delivery and when it is delivered.

SMS tracking notifications are available on the Growth and Scale plans. Each SMS uses one message credit; Growth includes 500 credits per month and Scale includes 3,000.

A branded tracking page with your logo and colors is available on the Scale plan only.

Tracking updates are pulled from carriers about every 2 hours.
